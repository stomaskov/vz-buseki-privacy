Privacy Policy for VŽ Buseki

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Incode, vl. Siniša Tomašković ("we", "our", or "us") operates the VŽ Buseki mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information when you use our App.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information Collection and Use

The App does not require registration and has no user accounts. We do not sell your information or share it with anyone for advertising.

Timetables, stops and routes are built into the App. Finding your nearest stop happens on your device, and your location is not sent anywhere for it. Information leaves your device only in the cases below.

Problem reports

If you choose to report a problem with a timetable, stop or route, the App sends us:

  • the message you write;
  • your email address, only if you enter one, so that we can reply;
  • your device's location, only if you tick the option to attach it;
  • which line, stop and date the report is about, and the App's version;
  • a random identifier created by Firebase Authentication, which is not linked to your name or to any account.

Reports are stored in Google Firebase Realtime Database and are used only to correct the App's data and, if you gave an email address, to answer you. Reports are not deleted automatically: a report is kept until we remove it, or until you ask us to (see below).

Trip planning

When you plan a trip, the App sends your current location, the stops on the trip and your destination to the Google Maps Directions API to draw the walking parts of the route. When you search for a destination, the text you type is sent to Google Places to suggest matching places in Varaždin County.

Map and diagnostics

The map is provided by the Google Maps SDK. Google states that the SDK collects device information (such as the operating system version and device model), crash reports from the SDK, your IP address, an identifier specific to the SDK, and how you pan and zoom the map. Google uses this to provide and improve its services.

Purchases

Any purchase in the App is handled by Google Play. We never receive your payment details.

All of this information is encrypted in transit.

Deleting Your Information

To have a problem report you sent deleted, write to us at the address below and tell us roughly when you sent it and what it said. Because the App has no accounts, this is how we find it.

Cookies

The App does not use cookies explicitly. However, third-party libraries used in the App may use cookies to collect information and improve their services.

Third-Party Service Providers

The App uses the following services. Their privacy policies describe how they handle the information above:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Realtime Database) – https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Maps Platform (Maps SDK, Places, Directions) – https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Play Services – https://policies.google.com/privacy

Data Security

We value your trust in providing us your information, thus we strive to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. But remember that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

Children’s Privacy

This App does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you discover that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we can take the necessary actions.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
